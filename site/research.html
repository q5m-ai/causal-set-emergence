When does a discrete action recover geometry?

A one-page account, exact claims and a route into independent review.

Prepared, not launched or submitted. Written claims await independent human scrutiny. Selected formal results do not certify this entire package.

The question

Could spacetime be made of discrete events, ordered by which can influence which? This project tests a narrower consistency question. Start with a specified smooth spacetime, scatter events into it randomly, and count pairs according to how many events lie between them. Does the averaged gravitational action approach the expected curvature and boundary expression as the events become denser?

That is not deriving spacetime, gravity or matter from a microscopic theory: the background geometry is supplied at the start.

What the research reports

The strongest negative claim is a complete-action counterexample in four spacetime dimensions. A fixed smooth curved universe between two spacelike time slices has zero proposed curvature-plus-joint target, but its action tends to negative infinity in the written proof. All pairs and the point term remain; this is not just a divergent estimate at one ray.

A corrected flat-spacetime theorem covers every member of the stated smooth two-spacelike-face class in dimensions two through four, with counterexamples in each higher dimension. That does not mean every higher-dimensional region fails. Positive curved results include the whole smooth 2D spacelike core and a 4D family retaining focusing. Focusing alone predicts neither success nor failure.

Why it is difficult

Widely separated, nearly lightlike events can enclose tiny spacetime volumes. Their contributions need not disappear at high density. Signed counting weights cancel ordinary terms, but a particular focusing geometry can leave a fractional term. Proving that it survives the whole integral, including opening and closing contacts, is the crucial step.

Limitations

The central general arguments are written proofs, not fully Lean-certified results. An arithmetic certificate establishes one model coefficient’s sign, not its physical transfer. Independent human acceptance and novelty review remain outstanding. General curved 3D, broader corrected curved coverage and general null/mixed boundaries remain open. These are expectations, not convergence of individual samples.

The mathematics can be scrutinized without solving every open theorem. It does not establish an observed propagation anomaly or falsify causal-set physics as a whole. The separate experimental assessment returns B: a missing model-to-observable calculation. Experiment is not a publication prerequisite or an added condition for closing issue #24.

A proof spine readers can challenge

  1. Define independently. Physical interval volume and Poisson counting give the actual signed kernel. Geometry supplies curvature, area and angle before any limit.
  2. Split at one fixed cutoff. S30 controls the short part, with point once, face compensation and joint. All long nearly-null partners remain.
  3. Use signed cancellation. In 4D, three exact moments vanish. A derived summable cubic primitive is sufficient for zero long response; an absolute bound before subtraction is not.
  4. Resolve the geometry. Flat lift unions and the positive sphere family supply their own critical estimates. CO’s quartic focus instead produces a physical Mellin pole at 9/7 that the kernel does not cancel.
  5. Match the whole action. Both wells, both tips, opening/closing contacts, regular complements and cutoff tails must be restored. Only then does the negative pair factor yield CO’s divergence.
  6. Transfer the expectation last. The finite-density bridge identifies the mean. It does not prove a limit by itself or supply random-sample convergence.

Highest-risk steps: exhaustive minimizing-path classification; CO5–CO13’s uniform physical transfer and closing-fibre matching; CO14–CO16’s complete Mellin continuation; FLAT’s faithful development and moving masks; SW’s signed sphere map and domination; the shared written S30 theorem. The model-sign certificate alone cannot settle these.

Experiment: a separate assessment, outcome B

The missing calculation is a model-to-detector response. The action theorem selects neither a matter operator nor an astrophysical background. It supplies no photon speed, graviton damping or diffusion coefficient.

The concrete DHS scalar link model preserves ordinary dispersion and estimates a fractional RMS upper scale near 9×10⁻¹³ for its example. PDS energy diffusion offers a better-supported spectral observable with historical CMB bounds near 10⁻⁴⁴–10⁻⁴³ J/s, but its free parameters are not derived here. A recent LISA result is a conditional forecast, not present observational evidence.

Public FIRAS spectra, GWOSC/FERMI entry points and COSMOGRAIL/CDS light-curve schemas were checked without bulk downloads. Source lags, calibration, foregrounds, covariance, lens models and microlensing prevent treating raw precision as a model test. Focusing in CO is not a predicted lensing anomaly.

Read the primary-source assessment, quantified comparisons, datasets and exact missing calculation. Mathematical counterexample, physical-model falsification and empirical support for causal sets are three different things. No major experiment is proposed.
