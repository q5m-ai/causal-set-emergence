01 · Foundations

Three pillars

Strip away coordinates, smooth geometry, and even the familiar quantum state. What remains is a spare proposal for the architecture beneath spacetime.

I

Discrete events

A causal set is locally finite. Between any related pair lie only finitely many elements. A finite-volume region contains finitely many events—even though the whole universe need not be finite.

number ≈ spacetime volume
II

Partial order

A ≺ B says A is in B’s causal past. The relation is transitive, but not every pair is comparable: spacelike events sit outside one another’s light cones.

A ≺ B ≺ C ⇒ A ≺ C
III

Quantum histories

A prospective dynamics combines amplitudes for possible causal sets or growth histories. “Sum over worlds” is an intuition; “sum over histories” is more precise.

amplitude = Σ histories eⁱˢ

Why the long links?
A link contains no intermediate sprinkled event. Near a light cone, two events can be far apart in these coordinates but separated by little proper time.

This finite 1+1-dimensional illustration teaches the relation; it does not display the literal infinity of links possible in an unbounded Lorentzian sprinkling. A causal relation marks possible influence, not an actual interaction law.

04 · Quantum descriptions

Several roads in

“Quantize the theory” is not a unique recipe. Familiar formulations can agree under the right assumptions—but there is no universal theorem making every route equivalent.

Canonical

Classical quantities become operators acting on state vectors in a Hilbert space. Time evolution carries a state from one spatial slice to another.

Path integral

Assign an amplitude to each possible history, then combine them. Interference—not ordinary probability addition—is essential.

Stochastic

Add an auxiliary stochastic time and seek an equilibrium distribution reproducing a Euclidean quantum field theory.

≠

Agreement is conditional, not automatic.

Infinite degrees of freedom, gauge constraints, anomalies, topology, boundary conditions, and regularization can obstruct equivalence. A histories formulation may also be more natural when spacetime itself fluctuates.

This repo studies a precise version of the emergence question. Start with the four-dimensional causal-set action, average over Poisson sprinklings, and increase the density ρ. For restricted flat-spacetime regions, the calculation approaches a continuum joint-area term.
