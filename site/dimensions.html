    Construction scaffolding, not fundamental data. The axes, coordinates, spatial plane, and light cones show how an order is embedded in a continuum spacetime. The compass-point example does not require 3D. Select Witness to see a different six-event order that cannot fit in 1+1 dimensions but does fit in 2+1. In 1+1 dimensions, null coordinates turn causality into the intersection of two linear orderings; the witness requires three, so no rearrangement along one spatial line can preserve all six relations and all three non-relations.
