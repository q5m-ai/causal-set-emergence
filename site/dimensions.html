    Construction scaffolding, not fundamental data. The axes, coordinates, spatial plane, and light cones show how this example is embedded in a continuum spacetime. A bare causal set retains only the events and their order. This six-event order alone is too small to establish dimension; dimension is inferred statistically from a much larger causal set.
