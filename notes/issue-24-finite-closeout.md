These are complete-action claims, not failed estimates. They do not say every
region in those dimensions fails. Applying them to the published conjecture
still requires scrutiny of the proof and intended class/normalization, notably
empty joints, topology and the isolated/ambient interval regime. G-SS is the
repository's precise subcase, not a verbatim published admissibility predicate.

[Theorem FLAT / CFT4](corrected-flat-theorem.md) says the universal statement
for **every flat G-SS member** is true **if and only if** the dimension is 2, 3
or 4. Its negative clause is existential in each excluded dimension.
[C2.1–C2.18](curved-two-d-limit.md) separately prove the full curvature-plus-
counted-joint deterministic and expected limit for **every curved 2D G-SS
member**, without additional chart, topology or width restrictions. These are

acceptance. The retained all-dimensional Minkowski theorem and curved
SW/FCDC/PW/HO sufficient classes are not a general arbitrary-metric replacement.
The exact decomposition into target plus actual long response plus a vanishing
short error is useful, but an equivalent unproved long-limit condition is not
geometric resolution of the conjecture.

corrected curved/dimensional obligation remains #81; the successful classes
