# Null/mixed research handoff for PR #184

## What this PR is for

PR #184 preserves the null/mixed investigation as a **bounded research package**:
scoped written results, complete-action obstructions in the stated families,
finite diagnostics, source audits, and a precise record of unsuccessful general
constructions. The user has ended this line of investigation in favor of another
approach and requested that these artifacts be prepared for integration.

This is a change to the **PR's deliverable**, not to the original mathematical
acceptance of #156, #81 or #24. Reviewing or merging the package does not establish
the corrected general theorem, endorse every written argument independently, or
close those issues. The branch remains `issue-156-general-null-accounting`,
targeting `issue-81-general-coverage`, not `main`. Readiness for review is not
merge authorization.

The final research attempt is [GA](null-general-theorem-attempt.md), delivered
at `3e24b1a95487732f744d8d0f987282da5058f604`. Its outcome is **C / STILL BLOCKED**:
no independently constructed universal correction or complete general critical
remainder was obtained. The failed approaches are not a proof of impossibility.
This handoff adds no mathematical claim or numerical result.

## What is preserved

- **Scoped positive results and full accounting.** The existing NF, TC, NX,
  NG, FL and DL arguments retain their exact metric, boundary and dimensional
  hypotheses. SE/CS retain the completed flat and conformal common-tip scopes;
  they do not become general intrinsic or non-common-tip results.
- **Complete obstructions and restricted corrections.** NP/IU retain the
  spherical focal mechanism and its growth-plus-finite conclusion. SM retains
  the distinct flat all-null quotient mechanism, its complete 5D extra charge
  and higher-dimensional leading growth. CT's stronger finite-remainder result
  applies to the existing 6D flat family only, not every higher dimension.
- **General reductions that are not a general theorem.** IN's conditional
  suspension pairing, SM's origin/homogeneous matching, GT's clock/coarea and
  density-jet results, and CT's critical Abel-factor replacement remain at
  their stated scopes. The unresolved geometric contact extraction is not
  relabeled a supplied hypothesis or a completed consequence of these tools.
- **Reproducible finite diagnostics.** The accompanying Python modules and
  tests check identities, normalization, conditional geometry, physical unions,
  closing behavior and known failure modes. Passing them is not proof of the
  unencoded uniform asymptotic estimates or independent mathematical acceptance.
- **Useful negative research.** GA records why compact minimizing-path geometry,
  signed conservation/valuation and spectral/operator organization did not
  discharge the missing construction. Retaining this record should prevent a
  new approach from repeating those unsupported inferences.

These are the contents of the cumulative PR, not results newly proved by this
packaging change. Existing proof bodies, hypotheses, equation numbers and
source-pinned validation receipts are preserved.

nonproduct/non-common-tip, merging, accumulating-contact and singular-end
regimes. The original whole physical interval, both endpoint measures, every
partner and ordered sector, point once, fixed physical cutoff, homogeneous
terms and matching tails must survive any replacement approach.

#156 retains the null producers; #81 retains the corrected general consumer;
#24's completion criterion is unchanged. #177 and #94/#86 remain unresolved,
and #185's claim-to-Lean mapping remains deferred. No issue is closed by this
package and no narrower theorem is substituted for original acceptance.

The canonical action, kernels, closed ambient order and original finite Poisson
law are unchanged. Completed deterministic conclusions and their separate
original-law expectation transfers retain their distinct status. No general
expectation asymptotic or sample-wise convergence is added.
