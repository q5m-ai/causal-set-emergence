## What the labels mean

- **WORKS:** a complete deterministic result under the exact stated hypotheses,
  followed separately by original-law expectation. A written proof is not a
  compiled proof or independent human acceptance.
- **FAILS:** an admitted complete-action counterexample with actual matching
  and every complement, not a bad bound or a selected-sector coefficient.
- **UNKNOWN:** the required conclusion has not been proved or refuted for the
  remaining class. Failure of a sufficient test puts no member in FAILS.
