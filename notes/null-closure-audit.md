## 7. Intrinsic correction: NP rules out a universal local-jet repair

The audit of NP yields a further **proved negative consequence**, not a new
observable. Fix the same sphere radius and torus length L and let the fixed
width T range over pi<T<L/2. Write gamma_N(T) for NP8's complete leading
coefficient. The formula in NP3, with u fixed, gives as T decreases to u

```math
\begin{aligned}
G_N(u;T)&=\frac{256\pi u\sqrt{2u}}{945}
                  (T-u)^{9/2}+o((T-u)^{9/2}),\\
\gamma_N(T)&=\frac{5\beta_7}{128}
             \frac{2\pi V_Y I}{3D}\,G_N(\pi;T)>0 .
\end{aligned}\tag{NA3}
```

**Derivation.** Set v=u+(T-u)*s in NP3. Its bracket is
(2/3)*(T-v)^3/v plus a uniform higher-order error on this shrinking finite
v interval. This follows either by Taylor's integral remainder in
log(T/v), or from its convergent series with positive coefficients beginning
at order three. The remaining factor is v*sqrt(v^2-u^2). Divide by
(T-u)^(9/2) and use dominated convergence on 0<=s<=1. The integral is
B(3/2,4)=32/315, giving exactly NA3. This is a separate study of the explicit
coefficient function, not an interchange of T with the density limit.

**Local-repair no-go theorem.** No universal additive expression consisting
only of bulk and boundary-stratum integrals of isometry-invariant local
finite-jet densities can reproduce NP's leading complete coefficient on this
family. The densities may depend on rho, the fixed cutoff and fixed common
parameters, but not on T as external information or on distances to remote
strata. Even allowing a fixed translation-invariant affine marking/measure
on each null face does not help.

Proof: the local metric/face/seam/joint germs are unchanged by translating
between corresponding points of different widths. A bulk integral is a
constant times T^2; a null-face integral is a constant times T under the
allowed fixed marking; each seam or NN-screen integral is constant. Higher
finite jets introduce no T dependence. Thus every such proposed expression
has the form b2(rho)*T^2+b1(rho)*T+b0(rho). If its rho^(-2/7)-scaled value
converged to gamma_N(T) for every fixed T, evaluating at three distinct widths
