### What softer coordinates actually repair

In the circular support control, put d=u-a for two ordinary tied global
minima, k for the primary stiffness, ell for the secondary stiffness, and b,c
for the primary cubic/quartic coefficients. Write k=kappa(a) along the cut;
all primes below differentiate in a. For a smooth support profile f, the
support coordinates are p=(f-double-prime minus 2k)n minus f-prime times the
unit tangent. Stationarity and equality of the two critical values give

```math
\begin{aligned}
\kappa'&=3b+\kappa\cot(d/2),&u'&=-\kappa/\ell,\\
\frac{db}{da}&=4c+\kappa/3,\\
\kappa''&=12c+3b\cot(d/2)
       +\left(\frac{3\kappa}{2}+\frac{\kappa^2}{2\ell}\right)
                       \csc^2(d/2).
\end{aligned}\tag{PF3.4}
```

For verification, differentiate the first line, use d-prime=u-prime minus
one, and substitute the second line. The apparent inverse-soft-stiffness
term is controlled when the **primary is the softer state**, k at most ell.
This removes that particular denominator on a fixed ordinary branch. It is
not a uniform estimate through changing state selections. The formulas are
for the support family, not an identification of its off-critical Hessians
or amplitudes with the actual proper arrivals in CR3.11/CR3.16.

At a triple switch there may be no continuing choice of a soft ordinary

### The vertex calculation still lacks an allocation theorem

Three equal **global** minima at labels 0,a,b in one fixed smooth state chart,
after subtracting their common value, give the six-root division

```math
\begin{aligned}
D(z)&=z^2(z-a)^2(z-b)^2G(z),&G&\ge0,\\
\|G\|_{C^0}&\le C\|D\|_{C^6},\\
k_0&=a^2b^2G(0),&k_a&=a^2(a-b)^2G(a),&
k_b&=b^2(b-a)^2G(b).
\end{aligned}\tag{PF3.5}
```

Repeated-node Hermite division expresses G as a sixth divided difference;
the simplex integral of the sixth derivative supplies the uniform bound.
The factors are nonnegative, so G is nonnegative through the removable
roots. This does not hold for arbitrary unequal near-minima after subtracting
only the primary value.

The scratch proposed a triple coefficient proportional to the geometric mean
of the three stiffnesses divided by all three terminal chord lengths, paid
by the largest of the three cut-slope jumps. Its scale algebra is consistent:
the cube of the coefficient divided by the product of those jumps is the
inverse square of the product of the chords. Together with PF3.5 and an
assumed common head below all three natural phase scales, that can bound a
**candidate** vertex budget. It still neither derives the physical numerator
coefficient nor proves a common head or a non-overcounting allocation to true
vertices. Moving two chosen small gaps to zero can encounter a fourth state,
lose a minimum, or hit the actual good-clock cap first. Equal-global-minimum
bounds cannot be consumed on those paths without a localization theorem.

A simple collective check rules out replacing that theorem by independent
pair corrections. For n equal leading amplitudes and arrivals, with the
fixed phase normalization absorbed only in this diagnostic,

```math
\begin{aligned}
T_n(w)&=w n^{-2/3},&T_1(w)&=w,\\
T_n(w)-T_1(w)&=w(n^{-2/3}-1),\\
\sum_{j=2}^n\{T_{\{1,j\}}(w)-T_1(w)\}
 &=(n-1)w(2^{-2/3}-1).
\end{aligned}\tag{PF3.6}
```

The true inverse deficit is bounded by w; the independent pair deficits
accumulate linearly with n. An exact telescoping sequence of *aggregate*
inverses is possible, but its increments then depend on all earlier states,
not just an ordinary two-state coefficient. This is an algebraic control,
not a density-dependent geometry, an action obstruction or proof that every
possible collective allocation fails.

## 3. Final approach B: signed cancellation after the full partition sum

PF3.3 removes the artificial arbitrary-weight and second-coefficient
requirements. To test whether the remaining signed cancellation follows
from the existing qualitative expansion, set u=log(1/t), e(u)=E(exp(-u)):

```math
\begin{aligned}
H(s)&=e^{4s}F''(e^s),\\
\int_0^\infty z^3F''(z)\{E(tz)-E(t)\}\,dz
 &=\int_{\mathbb R}H(s)\{e(u-s)-e(u)\}\,ds,\\
\int_{\mathbb R}H(s)\,ds&=0,&
\int_{\mathbb R}sH(s)\,ds&=2\Gamma(4/3)\ne0.
\end{aligned}\tag{PF3.7}
```

The first log moment is CR3.18's derivative at p=3. There is no extra zero
that annihilates an arbitrary slow translation modulus. Summing the
partitions removes artificial derivatives, not physical face restrictions
or changes in competing physical intervals. Exchanging source and target
in a future-directed pair integral does not create an opposite-signed copy
of the same ordered domain. No such cancellation identity was obtained.

The logical calibration can even retain positivity and the **entire linear
tail**. On 0<w at most w0=exp(-4), define an abstract ramp Q-star; beyond w0
extend it by its tangent line:

```math
\begin{aligned}
Q_*(w)&=w^2/2+w^3/\log(1/w)\quad(0\lt w\le w_0),\\
Q_*''(w)&=1+w\left(\frac6{\log(1/w)}
       +\frac5{\log^2(1/w)}+\frac2{\log^3(1/w)}\right)>0,\\
Q_*(w)&=Q_*(w_0)+(w-w_0)Q_*'(w_0)\quad(w>w_0),\\
\int_{\mathbb R}H(s)\{e_*(u-s)-e_*(u)\}\,ds
 &=\frac{2\Gamma(4/3)}{u^2}+O(u^{-3})\quad(u\longrightarrow\infty).
\end{aligned}\tag{PF3.8}
```

Here Q-star at zero and its right derivative are zero. Thus it is exactly
the ramp of the finite positive measure with density Q-star-double-prime
on (0,w0), zero outside. No phase tail has been deleted. Its first
coefficient is one, and e-star(u)=1/u for u at least four. To justify the
last line, expand 1/(u-s) on the region abs(s) at most u/2. H has all absolute
log moments; its negative tail decays exponentially and its positive tail
superexponentially. The omitted region, including the linear continuation,
is smaller than the stated error. The nonzero first log moment survives.
Consequently its signed response divided by square root t does not vanish.

This is **not a physical interval distribution or a G-SS counterexample**.
It only proves that first subtraction, a qualitative cubic little-o error,

The distinguished north pole has zero source measure. The construction

**Approach B stops here.** The exact signed reformulation is available, but
