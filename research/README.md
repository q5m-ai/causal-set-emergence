## One-page account

### The question

Could spacetime be made of discrete events, ordered by which can influence
which? Causal-set theory explores that possibility. This project studies a
narrower consistency question. Start with a **specified smooth spacetime**,
scatter events into it at random, and count pairs according to how many events
lie between them. Does the resulting gravitational action, averaged over those
random samples, approach the expected curvature and boundary expression as the
events become denser?

That direction matters: recovering an action on an already supplied spacetime
is not deriving spacetime, gravity, or matter from a microscopic theory.

### What the research reports

The strongest negative claim is a **complete-action counterexample in four
spacetime dimensions**. An explicit smooth, curved, compact spatial universe
between two spacelike time slices has zero proposed curvature-plus-joint target.
The written argument instead obtains an action tending to negative infinity.
It retains all pairs and the point term. It is not merely a divergent estimate
or a calculation at one exceptional ray.

A second result is a sharp corrected **flat-spacetime** statement: every member
of the specified smooth, compact-closure, two-spacelike-face class has the
proposed mean limit in dimensions two through four; each higher dimension
admits a counterexample. This does **not** say all higher-dimensional regions
fail. The repository also supplies written positive curved results, including
all members of that class in two dimensions and an infinite-dimensional
four-dimensional family that retains focusing. These successes and failures
are compatible: focusing alone is not a criterion for either outcome.

### Why the proof is difficult

Two events can be very far apart in coordinates but enclose very little
spacetime volume if their separation is nearly lightlike. Such pairs remain
important at high density. Positive and negative counting weights cancel
ordinary contributions; a special focusing geometry can produce a fractional
contribution that escapes those cancellations. The hard step is proving that
this contribution survives the **whole** physical integral, including opening
and closing contacts and every complementary region.

### What is not established

The central general results are conventional written proofs, not fully
Lean-certified theorems. Selected restricted limits and probability foundations
have existing Lean proofs; arithmetic certification proves one model coefficient's
sign, not the geometric transfer. No attributable independent human acceptance
was found in the inspected review issues. Novelty and some source provenance
remain unresolved. Arbitrary curved three-dimensional coverage, broader corrected
curved coverage, and general null/mixed boundaries remain open. The statements
concern expectations, not convergence of individual random samples.

These mathematical results are ready to be **examined without waiting for every
open theorem**. They neither falsify causal-set physics as a whole nor supply
an observed propagation anomaly. A separate experimental assessment returns
**B: a missing model-to-observable calculation**, not a proposed major experiment.
Experimental success is not a prerequisite for publishing this mathematics or
an additional closure condition for issue #24.
